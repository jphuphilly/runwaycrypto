Help — what everything does, and when to run it


What this is


Runway Cryptos scans the top 250 coins on CoinGecko for names that are trending up with room to keep going, restricted to what you can trade on Kraken, and sizes each trade against your challenge's drawdown floor. It is a shortlist tool, not a signal. The track record at the bottom is how you find out whether the shortlist has an edge.



When to run it (Eastern, weekdays)


TimePurposeWhat to do


8:45 AMSetup scanAdvanced · Setup = "Cup or higher lows" (or the 🌀 Coiling preset). Handles and coils form in thin overnight volume. Build a watchlist of 2–3 names. Do not enter.


9:45–10:15Trigger scanHighest-volume window of the day (US open + London). Look for watchlist names now within 3% of the high with a positive 1h, or a Coil break. Press 🕯 Confirm candles. Enter only on a setup with a ✓.


11:00 AMConfirmationA pick still on the list held through the first round of profit-taking. Add here only at smaller size. One that dropped off told you not to chase.


8–9 PMAsia read (optional)Log which names are building handles or coils for tomorrow's 8:45 scan. Reconnaissance, not a session to trade from the phone.


Skip 12–3 PM (daily lull — 1h readings are noise), weekends (half the volume, wicks hit stops), and the 15 minutes around CPI (8:30 AM) and FOMC (2:00 PM) — those spikes register as Extended and are hidden on purpose. The screener only sees a 1h move that started in the last 60 minutes, so scans an hour apart cover the morning; one scan catches whatever hour you looked.



Modes


Basic
Only 24h must be up; wide liquidity limits; extended names shown. If nothing passes, the 10 strongest tradeable names are shown as Near miss so the first run always produces a list. Use it to get oriented.


Advanced
All controls visible. Loads the tuned defaults: Short bull preset, 24h ≥ 2%, $1B cap, $100M volume, turnover ≥ 1.5%, within 3% of the 24h high, extended hidden. An empty table here is real information. Switching modes resets the filters; Reset restores the current mode's defaults and leaves your challenge fields alone.



Timeframe chips and presets


Each chip cycles off → up ▲ → down ▼. "Up" means the window's change is positive and at least the minimum % beside it (Advanced only). Presets: Short bull 1h·24h·7d up — the core momentum scan. Day bull 24h·7d — use when 1h is noisy. Long bull 7d·14d·30d — trend context. 🌀 Coiling turns momentum off and shows only coils and coil breaks. Short bear mirror image, for reference only. Clear turns all chips off.



Challenge row


Preset
Kraken Funded $10K (+12% pass / −3% fail, no daily limit), Kraken Prop Starter $5K or $100K (+10% / −6%, 3% daily loss measured from the balance at 00:30 UTC = 8:30 PM ET, 7:30 PM in winter). All floors are fixed from the starting balance. Custom lets you type your own.


Current balance
Update it as you trade — everything in the budget line keys off it.


Risk / trade $
Dollars you lose if the stop hits. Presets set it: $40 Funded, $25 Prop $5K, $450 Prop $100K. On Prop presets the budget line shows fees as a share of R (0.04% per side plus two days of funding) and the resulting breakeven win rate — about 36–40% depending on the stop — and the 2R target logged to the track record is raised by that fee.


Stop %
The flat stop distance used when a coin has no setup structure. Setups override it (see Stop %).


Daily loss % / balance at day start
Prop only. The budget line shows the tighter of the daily room and the total room.


Budget line: room above the floor in dollars and in max-risk losses (amber at 4, red at 2), dollars and R to the target, and today's remaining daily room if a daily limit is set.



Liquidity row (Advanced)


Universe
Kraken list only, or all of CoinGecko. Edit the Kraken symbols box to match what the app actually shows you; it is a guess.


Min mcap / Min 24h vol
In millions. Below $1B and $100M the majors list gets thin enough that a 2% stop gets hunted.


Vol / mcap ≥ %
Daily turnover. Majors run 1.5–4%; 8%+ means an event is on. Setting it high cuts BTC and ETH.


Within % of 24h high
The trigger gate. Tight (3%) wants coins holding the high; loosen to 10% to see pullbacks.


Within % of ATH
Overhead-supply proxy; 50% is deliberately loose. Blue sky uses it.


Min score
0 by default. Score is a ranking, not a gate — see Score.


Setup
Require a chart structure: cup & handle, cup or higher lows, blue sky, coiling, coil break.


BTC regime gate
On by default: no longs when Bitcoin's 24h change is below −2%. Every altcoin long fails together on a BTC selloff, so the gate removes the whole list rather than one coin at a time.


Extended
1h > 8% or 24h > 30%. Hidden by default; "Show" lists them with score 0. These are the moves that have already happened.


Auto refresh
Off / 1 / 5 / 15 min. Use 5 during the morning. 1 min all day will get you throttled.


CoinGecko demo key
Free at coingecko.com/en/api. Raises the limit from roughly 5–15 calls/min to 30 and ends the "Failed to fetch" problem. Not saved between page loads.



Stats bar


Scanned / Matched / Breakouts / Top score, then Eliminated by — how many coins each filter removed, in order. When the table is empty, this tells you which filter to loosen.



Columns

Change over the window. "—" means CoinGecko had no data (common on 1h for thin coins); a missing window fails an "up" chip rather than counting as 0.


Streak
How many of the five windows are up. It counts, it doesn't stop at the first miss.


vs 24h high
Distance below the 24h high. Green under 2%.


Vol/mcap · Mcap · Vol 24h
Turnover and size.


Rank
The default sort. Mechanical: valid entry first (Breakout), then tighter stop, fresher level (for coil breaks), higher turnover. It ranks by executable edge rather than by how far the coin has already run.


Score
0–100 ranking of realized momentum: 1h, 24h, 7d (each capped), turnover, ATH proximity, liquidity, 24h-high proximity, plus a setup bonus (cup 12, coil break 12, blue sky 8, higher lows 5). Extended names score 0. A 90+ means everything is already pinned — the coin has moved the most, not the one with the most left. Sort by it; don't gate on it.


Signal
Breakout — not extended, has a setup (cup, higher lows, blue sky or coil break; a plain coil is a watch item), and is either within 2% of the 24h high or a coil break. Score is not part of the gate. Logged to the track record. Strong ≥ 75. Watch ≥ 55. Extended and Near miss as above.


Setup
From the 7-day hourly sparkline. Cup & handle: a rim at least 24h old, a 3–30% dip, recovery to within 4% of the rim, and a 6–24h handle under 4% range that held above the cup's midpoint. Higher lows: rising lows and highs across the week's thirds. Blue sky: above the 7d high and within 10% of ATH — nobody underwater above. Coiling: the longest 48–120h window with ≤ 6% range that is clearly tighter than the week; a watchlist item, set an alert at the top. Coil break: cleared the coil top in the last 6h and less than 6% above it — the trade. Hover any badge for the levels. Seven days of data catches a fast pattern only; a three-week base won't register.


Candle
Filled by 🕯 Confirm candles (top 12 rows, ~20 s). Bullish pattern in the last 3 completed hourly candles: three white soldiers, morning star, bullish engulfing, piercing line, hammer. ✓ means it printed at the setup's level (handle low, coil bottom, last higher low, or a close through the rim/coil top). A pattern without the check is shown but means much less.


Stop %
Structural where there is structure: just under the handle low (cup), under the coil bottom (coil / coil break), under the last higher low (trend, capped at 2× your flat stop), otherwise the flat Stop %. Floored at 1% so a tight handle can't produce a wick-bait stop. A coil break stop is wide on purpose — the bottom is what invalidates the trade.


Size @ risk
Risk ÷ stop distance, capped at 20% of balance per position ("cap" shows when the cap bound, and the hover shows the smaller risk that results). Gear shift: when the room to the floor is 3 max-risk losses or less, risk is automatically halved and the budget line says so in red. Keep total exposure under 40% — two capped positions. A correlated dump with slippage is what the cap is for.


7d
Sparkline of the week.



Track record


Every Breakout from a live run is logged once with its flag price, structural stop, 2R target and +12% mark. Later runs update last/max/min and settle each pick: Hit 2R (reached 2R before the stop), Stopped (stop first), Expired (open after 3 days). Verify with history pulls hourly candles for open picks and settles them in order, so it knows which level hit first even if you weren't watching; after a 2R win it keeps tracking the high so +12% still registers. Picks where the stop and 2R were both touched in one bar or between two samples are marked Ambiguous and excluded from the rate — never guessed. Verify uses Kraken's own 1/5/15-minute candles when the browser can reach api.kraken.com (hover a status to see the source) and falls back to CoinGecko hourly closes, which miss wicks and flatter the result. The summary shows the number that matters — 2R-before-stop rate with a 95% confidence interval and a count toward 100 settled picks — split by score bucket (90+, 75–89, under 75). Breakeven is about 33% with no fees and about 37% on Prop. The test is not "is the rate above breakeven" but "is the low end of the interval above breakeven" — at 20 picks the interval is ±20 points, so nothing is proven until well past 50. Until then: freeze the thresholds, trade small, and log. The log lives in this browser's storage for this file: open the same copy in the same browser, and Export weekly.



URL parameters (for Spark / scheduled agents)


Open the hosted page with settings in the address so an agent doesn't have to click: ?mode=advanced&preset=funded&setup=cuptrend&bal=10776&run=1&open=1. Supported: mode basic|advanced · preset funded|prop5|prop100 · tfpreset short|day|long|coil · chips e.g. 24h,7d · setup any|cup|cuptrend|sky|coil|coilbreak|coilany · bal current balance · risk · minscore · nearhigh · btcgate · key CoinGecko demo key · run=1 auto-run · open=1 expand the "Results as text" block. That block is a plain-text table an agent can read or paste into a Sheet; the Copy button puts it on the clipboard. Note: an agent's remote browser usually starts with empty storage, so the in-page track record won't persist there — the Sheet the agent writes to becomes the log.


Keyboard and errors


R re-runs. "Failed to fetch" with no HTTP code is usually CoinGecko throttling without CORS headers; the page retries at 15/30/45 s, then tells you whether it's throttling or a blocker on api.coingecko.com. A hosted/preview copy of this page can't reach the API at all — use the downloaded file for live scans.
